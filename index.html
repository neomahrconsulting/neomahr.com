Why Neoma Exists

HR outsourcing companies and PEOs serve an important purpose. For the right organization, they are transformative. But they are not the right fit for everyone, and they are rarely the ones who will tell you that.

Before you sign a contract, restructure your HR function, or hand off your people operations to a third party, you deserve an honest assessment from someone without a stake in the outcome.

That is exactly what Neoma HR Consulting provides.

We work with small and mid-size businesses, nonprofits, and HR leaders to assess what is actually happening, advise on what kind of support will genuinely help, and connect you with the right solution. Sometimes that is strategic consulting. Sometimes it is an HR executive search. Sometimes it is an outsourcing partner or a specialized consultant from our vetted network.

Neoma HR Consulting is not in the business of selling you something. We are in the business of telling you what fits.
