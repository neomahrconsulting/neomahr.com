The Neoma Referral Circle

Curated. Vetted. Trusted.

No HR consultant can be everything to every client. Pretending otherwise is how organizations end up with the wrong help.

The Neoma Referral Circle is a small, intentional group of independent HR consultants Neoma trusts to do excellent work in their areas of specialty. When a client’s needs fall outside Neoma’s expertise, the Circle is where the work goes.

Every member of the Circle is personally vetted by Adrienne. Every referral is matched to the client’s actual need, not the available bench.

For Clients

Looking for the right specialist?

If you are looking for HR support and want to be matched with the right specialist, start with Neoma. We will assess your situation, advise on what kind of expertise will help, and connect you with the consultant best suited to do the work.

You will not be sold to. You will not be passed off. You will be guided.

For Consultants

Interested in the Circle?

Membership in the Neoma Referral Circle is invitation-based. We are looking for independent HR consultants with deep specialty expertise, a track record of excellent client work, and a commitment to honest, conflict-free advising.

If that sounds like you, we would like to know more.
